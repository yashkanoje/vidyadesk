About BrightPath

BrightPath Classes has prepared students in Rourkela for JEE and NEET since 2011. Classes are held at Plot 12, Civil Township, Rourkela. Batches are small, with at most 40 students in a classroom, so every student gets attention from the teachers.

Faculty

Physics is taught by Mr. S. Mohanty, who has 14 years of teaching experience. Chemistry is taught by Dr. P. Das. Mathematics is taught by Mr. A. Pattnaik, and Biology for NEET students by Dr. R. Mishra. All faculty members are full-time teachers at BrightPath.

BrightPath Talent Search Exam (BTSE)

The BrightPath Talent Search Exam is a scholarship test held on the second Sunday of every month from April to July. Students of Class 8 to Class 12 can take it. The registration fee for the exam is 200 rupees. The top ten rank holders receive a fee waiver of up to 50 percent on the course fee, and ranks 11 to 50 receive a 20 percent waiver. Results are announced one week after the exam.

Refund policy

A student who withdraws within 7 days of admission gets a full refund of the fee paid. A student who withdraws within 30 days gets 50 percent of the fee back. No refund is given after 30 days from the date of admission. Refunds are paid by bank transfer within 15 working days.

Transport

BrightPath runs its own buses on four routes: Sector 19, Udit Nagar, Chhend Colony and Bisra Road. The bus fee is 1,200 rupees per month. Buses leave the institute 15 minutes after the last class of each batch.

Tests and study material

Every batch writes a chapter test every Sunday morning. An all-India mock test is held on the last Sunday of every month, and students get a detailed report with their rank. Printed study material and an online practice portal are included in the course fee.

Doubt clearing

Doubt-clearing sessions are held every Saturday from 3 pm to 5 pm. Students can also post doubts on the online portal, and a teacher answers within 24 hours.

Attendance and parent meetings

Students must keep at least 85 percent attendance. Parents receive an SMS when a student is absent. A parent-teacher meeting is held on the first Saturday of every month at 11 am.

Results in 2025

In 2025, 42 BrightPath students qualified for JEE Advanced and 118 students qualified in NEET. The top rank holder from Rourkela, Ananya Sahu, secured an all-India rank of 1,240 in JEE Advanced.

Admission documents

For admission, please bring the mark sheet of the last class passed, two passport-size photographs and a copy of the student's Aadhaar card. Admission is confirmed only after the first instalment of the fee is paid at the office.

Holidays

The institute is closed for ten days during Durga Puja and one week at Diwali. The holiday list for the year is shared at admission.
